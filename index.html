Why cakes go wrong

Most cake problems come from three causes. Each one has a plain fix.

Dry cake

Too much flour, or too long in the oven.

Fix: spoon the flour into the cup and level it off. Check the cake 5 minutes before the stated time. Brush warm layers with simple syrup.

Flat cake

Old baking powder, or too much mixing.

Fix: test the powder in hot water. It should bubble. Mix the batter only until it comes together.

Sunken centre

Underbaked, or the oven door opened too early.

Fix: do not open the oven for the first 20 minutes. Use the toothpick test before you take it out.

Three mistakes to avoid. Scooping flour straight from the bag packs it down, so you use too much. Cold butter and eggs do not cream well, so pull them out of the fridge 30 to 60 minutes early. Frosting a warm cake melts the frosting, so wait until the cake is fully cool.

How a good cake works

Three steps sit behind every cake in the collection. Get these right and the recipe takes care of the rest.

  1. Measure Weigh the flour on a kitchen scale. If you use cups, spoon the flour in and level it off. Never scoop from the bag.
  2. Mix Cream the butter and sugar for 3 to 4 minutes. Add the eggs one at a time. Mix the batter only until it comes together.
  3. Bake Preheat the oven for at least 15 minutes. Use the middle rack. Test 5 minutes before the stated time with a toothpick.

Bake your first cake in 5 steps

New to baking? Start here. These five steps come before every cake recipe on the site.

  1. Read the whole recipe first Read it once from start to finish, so nothing surprises you halfway through.
  2. Bring butter and eggs to room temperature Pull them out of the fridge 30 to 60 minutes early.
  3. Preheat the oven and line the pan Preheat for at least 15 minutes. Grease the pan and line it with baking paper.
  4. Measure everything before you mix Weigh or measure all the ingredients first, so you are not hunting for them with wet hands.
  5. Set a timer 5 minutes early Test with a toothpick before the stated time ends, so the cake never overbakes.

Oven, pressure cooker or microwave?

You can bake a cake without an oven. The method changes the time and the texture, so pick the one that fits your kitchen.

MethodTimeTextureBest for
Oven30 to 60 minEven rise, firm crumbFull-size cakes
Pressure cooker40 to 50 minSoft and moist, close to oven-bakedA full cake with no oven
Microwave90 seconds to 7 minSoft, best eaten at onceMug cakes and small cakes
Stovetop pan30 to 40 minEven bake on low heatNo oven and no cooker

For the pressure cooker, take out the rubber gasket and the whistle. Put 1.5 cups of sand or salt at the bottom with a stand inside. Preheat on medium heat for 5 minutes, then cook the cake on low heat for 40 to 50 minutes. Check with a toothpick. A clean toothpick means the cake is done.

Your first recipe: chocolate mug cake

This is the fastest cake in the collection. One mug, one fork, 5 ingredients, 90 seconds.

Chocolate mug cake, ready in 90 seconds

Prep: 5 min  |  Cook: 90 seconds  |  Serves: 1  |  Level: Easy

Ingredients

  • 4 tbsp plain flour
  • 4 tbsp sugar
  • 2 tbsp cocoa powder
  • 1 egg
  • 3 tbsp milk
  • 3 tbsp vegetable oil
  • Pinch of salt

Method

  1. Mix everything in a large mug until smooth.
  2. Microwave on high for 90 seconds.
  3. Let it cool for 1 minute before eating. Top with ice cream or whipped cream if you like.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest cake recipe for a first-time baker?

A mug cake. 5 ingredients, 90 seconds in the microwave. After that, try banana cake or a vanilla sheet cake.

Why does my cake come out dry?

Too much flour, or overbaking. Use the spoon-and-level method and check the cake 5 minutes before the recipe time ends.

Can I make a cake without an oven?

Yes. A pressure cooker, microwave, or deep stovetop pan all work. The pressure cooker gives the best texture for a full-size cake.

What makes a cake moist?

Use oil instead of butter, add yogurt or buttermilk, and never overbake. Brush warm layers with simple syrup for extra moisture.

How do I substitute eggs in a cake recipe?

Use 60 ml applesauce, 60 ml plain yogurt, or a flax egg, 1 tbsp ground flaxseed mixed with 3 tbsp water. For chocolate cake, 1 tsp vinegar with a quarter tsp baking soda works best.

Why did my cake sink in the middle?

The cake was underbaked, or the oven door opened too early. Never open the oven in the first 20 minutes.
